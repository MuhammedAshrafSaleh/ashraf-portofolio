The problem

CHR's properties were spread across PDFs, WhatsApp messages, and brochures.

The solution

Built a full-stack Laravel platform: a public marketing site backed by a resource-based admin dashboard managing every piece of content.

Every page had to mirror correctly in Arabic without maintaining two versions.

Nearly 20 content areas needed admin CRUD without duplicating boilerplate.
