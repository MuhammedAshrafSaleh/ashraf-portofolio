The problem

An Egyptian lighting and electrical supplier had no digital presence: the catalog lived in WhatsApp messages and PDFs, quotes were written by hand, and payments were tracked in notebooks. International e-commerce platforms offered no RTL support, no Egyptian payment gateways, and no understanding of a market where WhatsApp is the sales floor.

The solution

Built DOTS from scratch on Laravel 13 and Livewire 4 — a fully bilingual (AR/EN) RTL-native platform covering the full customer journey: discovery, catalog, cart, checkout, three Egyptian payment methods, order tracking, and a complete back-office, with WhatsApp woven into every step as the primary sales channel.

Key features
01

Arabic-first, RTL-native — Arabic-script URLs, bilingual sitemap, hreflang, language switcher that keeps page context

02

Livewire-powered product listing — faceted filtering by category, brand, attributes, price range; 4 sort modes; grid/list toggle; shareable filter URLs

03

Three Egyptian payment methods: Cash on Delivery, Paymob card checkout, and InstaPay via WhatsApp — with automatic stock reservation and release

04

WhatsApp as a first-class channel — every click logged (source, product, cart, order), bilingual editable templates, cart quoting in one tap

05

Full custom admin panel — products, variants, categories, orders, customers, coupons, delivery methods, payment methods, blog, services, projects, SEO, settings

06

565 automated tests — unit and feature coverage for checkout, payments, coupons, delivery rules, stock, WhatsApp logging, auth, and guard separation

Challenges I solved
01

75× faster product listing

Full-table scans on 10,000+ products brought listing pages to 15 ms. Added sort indexes to eliminate scans and Redis tag-based caching with precise invalidation on data changes — pages now respond in 0.2 ms. Zero N+1 queries enforced in development.

02

InstaPay — a payment flow with no API

InstaPay has no developer API. Built a manual-confirmation flow: order is created and items reserved, customer is guided to send a payment screenshot on WhatsApp, staff confirms in the admin. A scheduler releases reserved stock on expired windows automatically.

03

Bilingual everything — including URLs

Arabic isn't a translation layer — it's a first-class locale with its own slug column, its own routes, Arabic-script URLs (e.g. /ar/منتجات/اسم-المنتج), and a bilingual sitemap.xml. Every admin field has side-by-side AR/EN editors — one save updates both languages.
